MyFolioVault — Indian Investment Portfolio Tracker

A self-hosted, 100% offline personal wealth management and statutory tax compliance application.

🚀 Live Application & Open Source ✨ Interactive Demo Ready

MyFolioVault

Tailored specifically for the Indian financial market, MyFolioVault runs completely privately on a local machine with zero external CDN dependencies and zero tracking. It tracks multi-broker holdings, computes accurate weighted average pricing (WAP), models Indian capital gains, and manages statutory tax nuances across Equities, REITs/InvITs, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), ETFs, Mutual Funds, and NPS Tier I & II.

PHP 8.1+ CodeIgniter 4 MySQL / MariaDB Bootstrap 5.3.3 (Offline) 100% Air-Gapped / Zero-CDN MIT License

📈 Equities & Corporate Actions

NSE/BSE multi-exchange support with automated bonus dilution, stock split multipliers, rights issue cost integration, and dividend ledgers.

🏢 REITs & InvITs Taxation

Specialized 4-component distribution ledger: dividend income, slab interest, capital repayment cost basis reduction, and other income.

🥇 Sovereign Gold Bonds & Debt

Gram-based SGB holdings with maturity tracking and Indian IT Act Section 47(viic) capital gains tax exemption logic upon RBI redemption.

💼 Mutual Funds & NPS PRAN

SIP and lump-sum investment ledger, ELSS 3-year statutory lock-in tracking, and PRAN-level multi-pension fund manager asset allocations.
